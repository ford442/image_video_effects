### Review fix round (glass-shatter, unanchored-review findings)
**1. One-frame shard re-pack at click age 3.0 s — fixed.** Cause (as reported): the fragmentation envelope `min(1.5·e^{−1.3·age},1)` was still ≈0.03 at the `age < 3` cutoff, so `warpQ` jumped 0.985 → 1 and the whole lattice re-packed (repro t = 2.99 vs 3.01: 11.96 % of pixels differ by > 0.03, max 0.495). Fixes, all in the click-age loop and the centre:
- envelope `fresh = smoothstep(0,0.10,age)·(1 − smoothstep(0.3,3.0,age))` — exactly 0 *with zero slope* at 3 s; the 0.1 s attack removes the one-frame onset (fragmentation used to appear at full strength in one frame); the amber front and hairline flare use `clickFront`, now multiplied by `ageFade = 1 − smoothstep(2,3,age)` (HEAD's `e^{−1.7·age}` was ≈0.006 at 3 s, so it also stepped);
- second step found while sweeping: the warp centre was a hard `select(mouse, clickPos, impactK > heldK)`, which teleports the lattice when a decaying click envelope crosses the held one with the cursor away from the click (≈70 % of pixels changed in one frame in a held+click sweep) → centre is now the envelope-weighted blend `(impactPos·impactK + mouse·heldK)/(impactK + heldK)`;
- state packing went from two texels to one (fewer loads; see cost).
Numbers (576 px, final shader, click at (0.3,0.5), `SWEEP` 0.02 s steps, t −0.04 … 3.5, % of pixels changed by > 0.03 between consecutive frames): **pair 2.99 → 3.01: 0.42 % (was 11.96 %)**; click only: onset (t < 0.12) max 43.8 % at 0.06 s (the 0.1 s attack, 5 frames), **t ≥ 0.12: max 6.4 % at 1.62 s, median 3.1 %**, 2.96–3.04 s ≤ 1.3 %; held + click (cursor 0.6,0.5 down): onset max 51.4 % at 0.08 s, **t ≥ 0.2: max 5.9 % at 2.16 s, median 2.3 %**, 2.96–3.04 s ≤ 1.1 %. The residual 2–6 %/frame is the continuous lattice relaxation itself (the whole point of the idea), no outlier frames. Controls (other shaders, no click) are < 1 %. **Press → release is still bit-identical to never-pressed** (120 frames/3 s, max |diff| 0.0; control "still pressed" differs by 0.76, so the test is not vacuous); poisoned-C / sentinel-collision seeds give the same stats as before.
**2. Cost.** Wall-clock on this box is useless as a gate right now (load 6–12 from other forks' renders; per-round HEAD:new ratios in my own interleaved runs ranged 0.5–1.7 for identical code). So I added a deterministic proxy: user-mode instructions of the whole render (`perf stat -e instructions:u`, follows the Chromium children), per frame = (I(40..60 frames) − I(8 frames))/n at 576 px — repeatable to ±1 % (HEAD 1091/1094/1093 over three runs).

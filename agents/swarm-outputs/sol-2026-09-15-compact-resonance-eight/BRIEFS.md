# Compact Generative Resonance Eight — Idea Cards

Selection rule: the smallest coherent cataloged generative shaders without a
dated native-ideas pass, excluding completed June/September cohorts, utilities,
and the known idea-rich metadata-only `gen-chronos-crystal-labyrinth`.

## `gen-aetherial-plasma-loom`

IDENTITY: A ray-integrated, FBM-warped plasma loom whose luminous ribbons twist
around a dark core and respond to the pointer.

KEEP VERBATIM: 3D value-noise/FBM family, density integration, radial ribbon
surface, Density / Flow Speed / Twist / Core Brightness slider roles.

ADD:
1. Counter-woven plasma ribbons — a second, phase-offset ribbon family crosses
   the original with opposite twist, producing a recognizable warp/weft loom.
2. Reconnection knots — anti-aligned crossings compress into white-hot knots
   with a cyan/magenta exhaust pair along the two ribbon tangents.

FORBID: replacing the loom with particles, generic click rings, pointer springs,
or using feedback texture data as audio.

A PACKING: ACES display RGBA.

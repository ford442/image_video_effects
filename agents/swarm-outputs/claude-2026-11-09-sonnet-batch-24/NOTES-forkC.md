### Ideas
1. **Re-fogging wipe memory** (lines 148–165, history, tactic 6 + 2). Fog mask persists in C.r. Regrows `sdt·(0.12 + 2.5·creep)` where `creep = max(max-of-4-taps(±3% of height) − fogPrev, 0)`; `mask = min(windowMask·(1−clickPulse), refog)`, so the cursor / a click clears at once and what it leaves re-fogs over seconds (fastest next to fog). HEAD's `mix(…, 0.08)` hysteresis forgot a wipe within ~1 frame. Measured (arc-then-hold cursor, 288 px alpha/fog panel): HEAD no trail; ablated no-refog = live window only; new: wiped swath behind the cursor at +0.5 s, half re-fogged at +2.5 s, gone at +5.5 s.
2. **Chromatic Beer–Lambert** (lines 21–23, 227–236) — **§12.2F Beer–Lambert**: `T = exp(−τ·(0.60, 1.00, 1.70))`; `1−T` per channel scales the in-scatter and veil, so the veil is cool and the transmitted photo warm; visible as a blue halo where the window grades into fog. First try (0.80/1.00/1.35) moved colour only ~4/255; Rayleigh λ⁻⁴ (0.5/1/2.2) is the physical limit, I used the milder 0.6/1/1.7 to keep the iridescent veil.
3. **Forward-scatter glare** (lines 36–37, 189–206) — **§12.2F Henyey–Greenstein**: 8 taps in two rings (ρ = 0.03, 0.09 frame heights, ring 2 rotated 45°), weights = HG phase (g = 0.6) at scatter angle atan(12ρ), gathering `tap·max(luma−0.6, 0)`; normalised, ×2.0, added into the veil so it rides (1−T)·fog and the clear window stays free of it. Visible as a soft halo around bright lights inside fog (ablation: 2.1k px >6/255 at 576 px with GLARE 1.1; 2.0 is stronger).
**Formula names:** Beer–Lambert (§12.2F), Henyey–Greenstein (§12.2F) — one table row on this file, shared only with fabric-zipper (Fork A) in the batch.

# `gen-chromatic-oracle-jelly`

- A drifting spatial swarm forms luminous bells, watching eyes, curling tentacles, and persistent wakes.
- Pointer position attracts nearby jellies, held drag paints a chromatic wake, and clicks trigger expanding oracle pulses.
- Four controls independently change swarm density, vertical drift, tentacle curl, and oracle palette/history.
- Semantic alpha and generated depth follow bells, rims, eyes, and tentacles.

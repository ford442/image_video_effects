# `gen-liquid-cathedral-dream`

- Repeating arch and spire fields melt through a smooth two-axis refraction conveyor.
- Pointer position attracts the liquid architecture, held drag refracts persistent glass, and clicks unfold petaled rose-window fronts.
- Four controls independently change architecture density, melt velocity, refraction/history, and stained-glass hue.
- Semantic alpha and generated depth follow the procedural architecture.

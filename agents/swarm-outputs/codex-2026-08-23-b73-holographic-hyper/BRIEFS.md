# Batch 73 — Holographic / Hyper generative upgrade briefs

## Contract

Upgrade the ten real generative IDs below without changing their saved
`updatedParams` values or renderer API. Every effect keeps bindings 0–12,
16x16x1 workgroups, a bounds guard, A-authoritative feedback, exact C history
loads, B/C writeback prohibition, three distinct plasma audio bands, semantic
alpha, depth, direct pointer position, held input, and timestamp-bounded click
response. Persistent scalar writes are limited to guarded single-writer slots
`extraBuffer[133..138]`.

  pointer spring, and fracture/edge/shock semantic coverage.

  direct intensity, speed, scale, and mouse-influence mappings; finite bursts.

  click impulses deform height and normal without gamma preconditioning.

- `gen-hopf-fibration-fiber-bundle`: direct rotation/thickness/bloom/drift
  controls, exact display persistence, and fiber-phase click blooms.
- `gen-hyper-bismuth-clockwork`: exact display history, hit/glow alpha, held
  torque, and timestamp-bounded toothed gear shockwaves.
- `gen-hyper-dimensional-bismuth-matrix`: guarded bass envelope at slot 133,
  exact display history, semantic alpha, and local crystal disruption fronts.
- `gen-hyper-dimensional-tesseract-labyrinth`: exact display persistence, ACES,

- `gen-hyper-labyrinth`: raw HDR neon/alpha ownership, plasma-band-only audio,

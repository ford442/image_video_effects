SHADER: gen-sentient-aether-flora-biosphere
IDENTITY (one sentence): a fast flythrough of a raymarched alien terrarium — twisted green stems crowned by magenta four-fold KIFS petal-spheres with subsurface glow, a drifting bioluminescent spore swarm and HDR growth-front trails, on a purple fog void.
KEEP VERBATIM: domain-repeated stem (twisted sdCylinder) + folded petal spheres with smin; petal_r pulse (sin time*3 + growth*2.5 + bloom*0.6); FloraCtx; the camera flythrough, cursor chase / pitch / twist, click growth-seed shockwaves; SSS approximation and colour-shift; bloom glow; fog; the whole spore swarm loop; velocity-advected feedback (prev = C at advPx, growthEnergy from luma); HDR feedback hdr = min(color,8)*0.85 + prev*0.85*0.5, capped 6; semantic alpha; depth = t/max_t; the four params (x=Intensity, y=Speed, z=Scale, w=Mouse Influence) and every existing mapping.
ADD (3 native ideas):
  1. Asynchronous blossoming + scalloped corollas — today every flower pulses in near-lockstep. A stateless per-cell hash gives each plant its own bud->open phase: buds pull their four petal spheres together and shrink; open flowers spread them wide and grow a 5-fold scalloped rim (cos lobes on the azimuth of each folded petal sphere, so the KIFS mirror makes the lobes symmetric). A field of flowers at different stages reads as a living terrarium, not a tiled clone. Extends the existing petal/KIFS/bloom motif; no new object.
  2. Stamen crown with glowing anthers — a real flower has a reproductive centre. Six splayed filaments (polar-repeated capsules) rise out of the corolla, their length and splay driven by the same per-cell openness; each ends in a warm gold anther (new material 3) that glows more the more open the flower is (emission scaled by Intensity, the bloom slider). Stem-material filaments smin into the stem; anthers smin into the head. Gives the "aether-spores" a visible source.
  3. Phototropic bow — stems currently all stand as identical straight twisted columns. Each stem now bows with height^2 toward the key light (l = (1,2,-1) horizontal) with a per-cell hashed deviation and a slow independent sway, carrying the head with it; the twist axis follows the bent stem. Breaks the clone-forest look while keeping the stems, twist and domain repetition.
FORBID on this file: spring+ripple stamp (the file already owns cursor chase, click growth seeds and kick bloom — do not add more), IQ cosine palette, extraBuffer state, reimagining as another creature/plant, sibling-file motifs (braided fibrils, venation on petals, hopper terraces, feather lanes, data packets travelling along the stem cylinders — so no sap/pulse bands on stems), ACES on stored history.
SILENT BUGS on the read path:
  - click shockwave ring used `pow((dist - age*2.6)*7.0, 2.0)` with a negative base inside the ring radius -> NaN -> shockVec/shockFlash NaN -> camera and ray direction NaN on every click. Fixed by squaring with rr*rr.
  - NOT fixed, noted: HEAD writes extraBuffer[133..137] (bass envelope, last kick, smoothed cursor, prev time), but those slots are zeroed every frame, so the read side always sees bassEnv=0, lastKick=0 (kickEnv ~ exp(-time*2.5) ~ 0, i.e. the bass-kick bloom never fires), smoothMouse=(0,0), dt=0.1. Left as-is per contract (never build on it; changing it would change the saved feel). New ideas are stateless and do not touch it.
A PACKING: raw HDR history, as HEAD documents it: A = (hdr.rgb, alpha) with hdr capped at 6.0; C is read as colour/history (prev.rgb, luma -> growthEnergy). ACES on writeTexture only, not on the stored history. The new ideas add to `color` before the feedback mix and write nothing new to A. dataTextureB untouched.

ADD (3 native ideas):

Plasma Intensity scales their gain.

NOTES / bugs: all four sliders already live (verified by grep). No pow-of-negative, no zero-C path, no ripple.w read, no dead fract mask on the read path. SHADER: gen-symbiotic-light-networks
IDENTITY (one sentence): a dark void crossed by two species of proximity-linked node networks (cyan-green and amber-pink) whose filaments carry travelling sinusoidal light pulses, compete or cooperate by the Competition slider, flare where they cross, and are seeded by the mouse.
KEEP VERBATIM: node positions (hash22 seeds, sin/cos drift with bass/mids/treble), birth/age ramp and Growth Phase scaling, proximity linking (maxDist rule, strength = 1 - d/maxDist), networkSegment (closest-point distance, pulse phase, taper, smoothstep core), nodeGlow, networkCompetition, bg noise, species colours, intersection mix, mouse seed pulse and its pull on species-1 nodes, flicker, vignette; the four params (x=Network Density, y=Competition, z=Light Intensity, w=Growth Phase) with their existing mappings; all four are already live and stay so. HEAD has no `params` block (only `updatedParams`) - left byte-exact.
ADD (4 native ideas):
  1. Hub-thickened tubes (Physarum flux) - slime-mould networks thicken the tubes that carry the most flux. Stateless node degree (neighbours inside the base link radius) makes hubs; a filament's width scales with the mean hub-ness of its two ends (up to about 2.4x) and node glow radius grows with the node's own degree. Density therefore now changes hierarchy as well as count.
  2. Evanescent leakage halo - a light-guiding filament is never perfectly confined; a soft Gaussian skirt of the tube's own pulse leaks into the void around every filament (width tied to the hub-thickened tube width, so hubs bleed more).
  3. Mycorrhizal exchange tint - "symbiotic" in the name: where one species' leakage halo overlaps the other's filament, the filament takes on some of the partner's hue, scaled by (1 - Competition). Slider now also sets how much colour is traded; at high Competition the species stay pure.
  4. Luciferin afterglow - bioluminescence lingers. Node flashes and the mouse seeding pulse are held in dataTextureC with 0.95 per-frame persistence (max(instant, prev*0.95)), so dragging the mouse leaves a fading spore trail and nodes smooth their pulse. Static look is unchanged (max of a constant = the constant).
FORBID on this file: spring+ripple stamp, IQ cosine palette, extraBuffer state, packets/data-cylinder motif, braided fibrils, venation, hopper terraces, feather lanes, anything from symbiotic-light-propagation-networks.wgsl, changing the two-species / proximity-link identity.
SILENT BUG / floor fixes on read path: HEAD wrote hard-clamped un-tonemapped colour and alpha 1.0 (now ACES + semantic alpha from light coverage); title header said "Propagation" (file id is Light Networks). No pow, no ripple.w, no dead mask. Node positions are now computed once per pixel into arrays (identical expressions) instead of recomputed inside the O(n^2) j-loop.
A PACKING: A.rgb = afterglow memory (linear, pre-ACES, pre-vignette seed light), A.a = display alpha; C read as .rgb only, exact textureLoad(dataTextureC, coord, 0), no early return (max() self-starts from zero-init). ACES on writeTexture only, never on stored A. Not display RGBA.

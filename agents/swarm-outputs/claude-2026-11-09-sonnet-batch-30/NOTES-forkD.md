## Trim round (vortex-warp-coupled, cost only — picture unchanged)
- Re-time (SwiftShader, 512 px, 16 frames, held cursor 0.4,0.6, JSON defaults, 5 interleaved rounds, load 2-3): HEAD median 35 → before-trim 51 (1.46×) → trimmed 45 (**1.29×**) [45, 44, 45, 44, 51]. (Earlier quieter runs: before 1.32-1.42×, after one/two steps 1.25-1.31×.)
- Changes, all output-identical: (1) `prevVel` = one `fetchC` at the pixel centre (the 4-tap bilerp there is the texel); `bilerpC` now clamps the two row/column indices once and gathers 4 corners. (2) Advect gather skipped when `prevVel == 0` (back-trace lands on its own texel). (3) The cursor-track loads and filter maths run only where `influence > 0` or on the two track texels (everything they feed is multiplied by `influence`). (4) Confinement maths skipped only when curl, grad|ω| AND vel are all exactly 0 (bit-exact; a first version that also skipped tiny nonzero vel changed the output by up to 0.7 — see below). (5) Specular hash/pow only where `influence > 0 && dens > 0`. (6) `quantF16` uses integer round-half-even on the f32 bits (no log2/exp2) — identical results.
- Equivalence vs the pre-trim file (copy in forkD/vortex_before_trim.wgsl): RAW dump max|Δ| = **0.0** (bit-identical) for idle, moving cursor, clicks, click+move at 192 px/60 frames and 240-frame stop-and-rest runs at viscosity 0.5 / 1, f32 and A16.
- **Observation (open):** the confinement loop is chaotic — a 1-ulp perturbation of one constant (0.5 → 0.50000006 in `curlHere`) changes a click scene by up to 0.67 (8 000 pixels > 1e-4) after 60 frames. Outputs after a stir will therefore differ pixel-wise between GPUs/compilers even though statistics (energy bound, decay to rest, parked parity) hold. Tolerances for any future equivalence test of this file must be statistical, not per-pixel, once fluid is active.

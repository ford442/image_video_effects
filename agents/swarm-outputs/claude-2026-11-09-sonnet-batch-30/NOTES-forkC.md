- Idea 3 edge-oriented dispersion (L109-L129): ±2-texel exact C.r gradient → stroke normal n and edge strength e; displacement stretched along n (×(1+e)) and damped along the rim tangent (×(1−0.5e)): `M = I + e(1.0·nnᵀ − 0.5·ttᵀ)`. **Deviation from the card:** the card said "blend direction toward ±∇"; that has a sign ambiguity that would flip R/B when the rotating direction crosses the normal, so I used the sign-invariant stretch (continuous in rotation and gradient; e = 0 is bit-exact HEAD). Worst-case displacement ≤ 2× HEAD's (still UV-clamped).

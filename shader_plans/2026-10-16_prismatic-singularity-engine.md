# New Shader Plan: Prismatic Singularity Engine

## Overview
A hyper-dense, spinning core of compressed chromatic energy that bends space-time and fragments light into spectral ribbons.

## Features
- Central singularity with extreme rotational distortion and event horizon rendering
- Spectral ribbons pulled into the gravity well using chromatic aberration techniques
- Complex multi-layered gyroid noise structure compressed near the center
- Audio-reactive horizon radius and rotational speed (plasmaBuffer integration)
- Localized space-time bending around mouse interaction

## Technical Implementation
- File: public/shaders/gen-prismatic-singularity-engine.wgsl
- Category: generative
- Tags: ["singularity", "gravity", "spectral", "gyroid", "audio-reactive"]
- Algorithm: Raymarching through a heavily twisted domain utilizing spherical wrapping and dense gyroid SDFs.

### Core Algorithm
The space is twisted using a rotational matrix dependent on the inverse distance to the origin, creating a whirlpool effect. The core SDF combines a smooth sphere with heavily scaled gyroid noise to simulate matter being shredded at the event horizon. Spectral ribbons are generated by offsetting the distance field for separate RGB channels during the lighting phase.

### Mouse Interaction
The mouse (`u.zoom_config.yz`) position serves as a secondary gravitational node. When clicked (`u.zoom_config.w > 0.5`), it creates a localized tear in the fabric, pulling the spectral ribbons towards the cursor position and distorting the underlying coordinate system.

### Color Mapping / Shading
Uses severe chromatic aberration, separating raymarching into three distinct color bands near the singularity. The background is a deep void, while the ribbons glow with high-intensity neon colors. Audio bass (`plasmaBuffer[0].x`) modulates the glow intensity and the size of the event horizon.

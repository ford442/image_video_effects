# New Shader Plan: Aether-Forged Nacreous Labyrinth

## Overview
A hyper-dimensional structure where organic, pearlescent membranes weave continuously through rigid, crystalline geometries, bathed in shifting bioluminescent aether. It contrasts the hard, mathematically precise facets of quantum structures with soft, flowing fluid dynamics that pulse and breathe.

## Features
- Procedural iridescence simulating nacreous (mother-of-pearl) light interference.
- Dual-domain raymarching blending smooth organic SDFs with sharp, angular fractal cuts.
- Ambient aether-flow simulated using multi-octave curl noise.
- Subsurface scattering approximation for soft, glowing organic tissues.
- Gravity-well mouse interactions that warp the spatial coordinate system dynamically.
- Audio-reactive blooming and pulsation tied to the membrane's volumetric density.
- Temporal history blending for ethereal motion trails and smooth light integration.

## Technical Implementation
- File: public/shaders/gen-aether-forged-nacreous-labyrinth.wgsl
- Category: generative
- Tags: ["organic", "crystalline", "iridescent", "raymarching", "aether", "bioluminescent"]
- Algorithm: Raymarching with domain warping, combining a KIFS (Kaleidoscopic Iterated Function System) fractal with a smooth volumetric density map driven by curl noise.

### Core Algorithm
The scene is built via a hybrid raymarching loop. Space is first subjected to a standard KIFS fold to create the rigid "forged" lattice structure. Inside this lattice, a secondary SDF calculates the "nacreous labyrinth"—a smooth, continuous surface generated by a triplanar-mapped gyroid and fBM (fractional Brownian motion) noise. The final distance field is a soft minimum (`smin`) blend of the sharp crystal and the soft organic web. Ray intersections calculate normals which drive the interference shading.

### Mouse Interaction
The mouse acts as a localized gravity singularity. Its coordinates (`u.zoom_config.y` and `u.zoom_config.z`) are mapped to world space. The distance from the ray origin to this point is used to apply a non-linear spatial twist and radial contraction to the space before the SDF is evaluated, effectively bending the labyrinth around the cursor.

### Color Mapping / Shading
The crystalline structures use a metallic PBR approximation with sharp specular highlights and chromatic aberration. The organic labyrinth utilizes a custom nacreous interference function based on the dot product of the view vector and the normal, mapping the result through a cosine palette to simulate thin-film interference. Subsurface scattering is faked using a secondary, blurred ray step into the volume, yielding a soft internal glow.

# New Shader Plan: Bioluminescent Chrono-Plasma Astro-Owl

## Overview
A hyper-dimensional, cyber-organic celestial owl forged from liquid chrono-plasma and shattered quantum-glass, soaring silently through a massive dark-matter volumetric nebula while radiating intense bioluminescence in sync with sub-bass acoustic frequencies.

## Features
- Ethereal, translucent fractal wings that leave lingering temporal echoes in the cosmic void.
- A core gravity well in the owl's chest that bends the surrounding aether, reacting to heavy acoustic bass drops.
- Volumetric particle-storm void background that responds dynamically to the wings' downstrokes.
- Eerie, glowing bioluminescent geometric eyes that scatter prismatic light through shattered quantum-glass lenses.
- Real-time fluid simulation of chrono-plasma feathers driven by fractal brownian motion (fBM) and chaotic domain warping.

## Technical Implementation
- File: public/shaders/gen-bioluminescent-chrono-plasma-astro-owl.wgsl
- Category: generative
- Tags: ["organic", "quantum", "cosmic", "volumetric", "fractal", "avian"]
- Algorithm: Advanced raymarching of a complex geometric bird SDF, combined with heavy domain warping for plasma feathers and a volumetric accumulation loop for the surrounding cosmic void.

### Core Algorithm
The owl's central structure is formed by a composite SDF of ellipsoids and tapered cylinders, blended with aggressive smooth-min functions. The plasma feathers are generated by applying a multi-octave 3D noise (fBM) domain warp to the base SDF, driven by temporal and acoustic uniforms (`u.config.x`, `plasmaBuffer[0].x`). The void uses an inverted noise accumulation loop for a deep, cloudy nebula effect.

### Mouse Interaction
The mouse coordinates (`u.zoom_config.y` and `u.zoom_config.z`) act as orbital gravity points, pulling the owl's gaze and shifting the focal point of the temporal particle-storm, effectively twisting the entire nebula around the viewer.

### Color Mapping / Shading
A vivid, ethereal palette ranging from bioluminescent cyan to deep quantum purple. Shading incorporates heavy fake subsurface scattering for the organic feather volume, crisp metallic specular highlights on the shattered glass lenses, and a massive bloom effect radiating from the core gravity well.

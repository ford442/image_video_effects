# New Shader Plan: Luminescent Nebula-Silk Weaver

## Overview
An expanding cosmos of silken energy ribbons that autonomously weave and untangle in zero gravity, driven by fluid dynamics and spectral analysis. This shader generates a mesmerizing, ethereal textile of starlight that breathes and flows, wrapping around unseen multidimensional objects and erupting into brilliant, audio-reactive flares.

## Features
- Ethereal, fluid-like ribbons generated via 4D curl noise and volumetric raymarching.
- Audio-reactive energy flares propagating through the silk strands based on bass and high-frequency inputs.
- Continuous multi-scale fractal domain twisting mimicking complex knot theory and zero-G fluid dynamics.
- Interactive localized gravity wells shaped by the mouse, pulling and smoothing the chaotic threads.
- Evolving iridescence and chromatic dispersion along the ribbon edges for a translucent, glassy feel.
- Bloom and subsurface scattering approximations to enhance the soft, nebulous illumination.

## Technical Implementation
- File: public/shaders/gen-luminescent-nebula-silk-weaver.wgsl
- Category: generative
- Tags: ["fluid", "ribbon", "nebula", "audio-reactive", "raymarching", "curl-noise"]
- Algorithm: Volumetric raymarching of dynamic 3D paths defined by curl noise and fractional Brownian motion (fBm), using distance-to-curve SDFs in a twisted domain.

### Core Algorithm
The environment relies on tracing paths along vector fields generated by a 4D curl noise function (using spatial coordinates and time). The scene calculates distance to these volumetric ribbons by projecting the ray position onto the nearest points of the vector field flows. fBM (Fractional Brownian Motion) is layered over the curl noise to add microscopic fraying to the ribbons. Density is accumulated during raymarching to create soft, overlapping translucency rather than hard surfaces.

### Mouse Interaction
The mouse (`zoom_config.yz`) acts as a localized repulsor or attractor field (a "spindle" that weaves the silk). When dragged, it creates a vortex in the underlying curl noise vector field. The ribbons dynamically wrap around the mouse coordinate in 3D space, tightening their weave and increasing their density locally, slowly returning to chaotic flows once the mouse is released.

### Color Mapping / Shading
The ribbons are rendered with a highly refractive, iridescent shading model. Base colors shift continuously based on spatial coordinates and time. The edges of the ribbons employ heavy fresnel equations coupled with chromatic aberration (splitting RGB refraction indices) to create a glassy, spectral edge. Audio data sampled from `dataTextureC` modulates the emission strength of the ribbons, sending bright, warm pulses of light (golds, intense magentas) flowing through the structure.
